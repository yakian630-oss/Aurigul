Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

At AURIGUL, your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website you accept the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly and information collected automatically when you browse:

  • Details you provide: name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing address, and order details when you create an account or check out.
  • Payment information: handled securely by our payment partners (e.g. cards, Easypaisa, JazzCash). We do not store your full card number on our servers.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, items added to cart, device and browser type, and cookies that keep your cart and preferences working.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and send order updates.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • To improve our products, website and shopping experience.
  • To send you offers and news — only if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to remember your cart, keep you signed in, and understand how the site is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features (like the cart) may not work properly without them.

4. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted partners who help us run our business — such as delivery couriers and payment processors — and only to the extent needed to serve you. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections at checkout. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can opt out of marketing emails at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@aurigul.pk or visit our Contact page. Our studio is at MM Alam Road, Gulberg III, Lahore, Pakistan.
