Our Story

Clothing with a conscience.

AURIGUL was founded in 2018 on a belief that good design and responsibility should never be at odds. We make considered, everyday pieces from natural fibres — built to be worn, loved and kept for years, not seasons.

How we work

Small batches, honest makers.

We partner with a handful of family-run mills and workshops who share our standards. Producing in small batches means less waste, better quality control and fair conditions for the people who make our clothes.

Every fabric is chosen for how it feels, how it wears and how it returns to the earth at the end of its long life.
